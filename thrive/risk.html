you can lose all the money you trade. only trade with money you can afford to lose. past performance, leaderboards and other traders' results do not guarantee future results.

leverage and liquidation

perpetuals let you trade with leverage. a small move against you can wipe out your margin. if your position reaches its liquidation price it is closed automatically and you lose the margin. higher leverage means a closer liquidation price.

memecoins and new tokens

many Solana tokens are new, illiquid or created to deceive buyers. prices can fall close to zero in minutes, developers can abandon a project, and you may be unable to sell. always check liquidity and price impact.

slippage and price impact

market orders and swaps fill at the best available price, which can differ from the price you saw. thrive limits slippage by default and warns you about large price impact, but fills are never guaranteed.

following and copying traders

other traders' trades are shown for information only. they may have different goals, capital, timing and risk tolerance, and they can be wrong. copying a trade does not copy their results.

protocol and technology risk

Hyperliquid, Jupiter, Solana, wallets and smart contracts can have bugs, outages, exploits or rule changes. data shown in thrive can be delayed or wrong.

keys and security

if someone gets your seed phrase they can take your funds. if someone gets your browser's trading key they can open and close positions (not withdraw). revoke unused keys in Hyperliquid → more → API.

regulation and taxes

crypto and derivatives may be restricted where you live, and gains may be taxable. you are responsible for following your local laws and reporting taxes.
