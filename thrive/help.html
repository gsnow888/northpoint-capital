what is thrive

thrive is a social trading terminal. you see every trade from real traders on the chart, follow the ones who win, get alerts when they trade and copy a move in one tap. market data is live from Hyperliquid and Solana.

your account

you don't need an account to use thrive. tap your avatar → sign in with an Ethereum wallet (MetaMask, Rabby…) or a Solana wallet (Phantom…). signing a message is free and never moves funds.

with an account you can pick a username, post theses on any market (long, short or neutral, up to 500 characters), like other traders' theses, appear on the thrive traders leaderboard, and sync your follows, favorites, practice account and layout across devices.

practice mode

every new visitor starts in practice mode with $10,000 of simulated money. prices are real; the money is not. your practice positions and history are saved only in your browser. you can add practice funds or reset the account at any time from deposit more.

trading for real

switch the top toggle from practice to real. a short setup guides you:

  1. accept the risk disclosure and confirm you are not in a restricted country.
  2. connect your wallet (MetaMask, Rabby or similar for perps; Phantom for Solana tokens).
  3. deposit USDC on Hyperliquid (minimum 5 USDC, from Arbitrum) if you want to trade perps, indices and stocks.
  4. authorize a trading key so you don't have to sign every order. it can open and close positions but cannot withdraw.

after that, every order you place is a real order on Hyperliquid or a real swap on Solana through Jupiter, from your own wallet.

markets

  • solana tokens: trending, new, top volume and graduated memecoins, with live trades and the wallets behind them.
  • perps: 170+ crypto perpetuals on Hyperliquid with up to 40× leverage.
  • indices & stocks: nasdaq 100, s&p 500, gold, oil and stocks like NVIDIA or Tesla as 24/7 perpetuals.

fees

perps, indices and stocks (Hyperliquid): Hyperliquid's own trading fee plus a thrive fee of up to 0.05% of the order size. you approve the maximum thrive fee once, with a signature, before your first real order.

solana tokens (Jupiter): network and priority fees paid in SOL, plus a thrive platform fee of up to 0.5% when enabled. the exact amount is shown before you confirm in your wallet.

practice mode is free.

safety

  • thrive is non-custodial: your funds stay in your wallet or your Hyperliquid account. thrive never holds them and cannot withdraw them.
  • the trading key is stored only in your browser. if you lose access to the device or want to revoke it, go to Hyperliquid → more → API and remove the key named thrive.
  • never share your seed phrase. thrive will never ask for it.
  • memecoins can lose most of their value in minutes. check liquidity and price impact before you buy.

install the app

thrive installs from the browser, no app store needed. on iPhone open it in Safari → share → add to home screen. on Android or desktop open it in Chrome or Edge → menu → install app.

keyboard shortcuts

/ search · b buy/long · s sell/short · 1–6 timeframe · l candles/line · f focus chart · [ ] hide panels · ↑ ↓ change market · 0 reset zoom · ? all shortcuts. drag the edges between panels to resize them.
