privacy policy

what thrive stores, what it shares and why.

last updated November 18, 2026

draft · this document is a working draft pending review by legal counsel. it is not yet the final legal text.

the short version

you can use thrive without an account. if you choose to sign in, you do it with your wallet (no email, no password) and we store only what's needed for the social features. your trading key never leaves your browser. we don't sell data.

if you sign in (optional)

signing in uses a wallet signature (Sign-In with Ethereum / Sign-In with Solana). our database provider, Supabase, stores:

  • your wallet address and chain, and the username you choose
  • the theses you post and the likes you give — these are public
  • your synced settings: follows, favorites, practice account and layout — these are private to you

you can delete a thesis at any time. to delete your account and all its data, contact us (see below).

what stays in your browser

  • practice mode balance, positions and history
  • traders you follow, favorites, alerts and layout preferences
  • your connected wallet addresses and, if you enable real trading, your trading key (never sent to our database)

you can delete all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser.

what third parties receive

to show live data and execute trades, your browser talks directly to:

  • Hyperliquid (market data, positions, orders) — receives your wallet address and signed orders.
  • Jupiter and the Solana network (quotes, swaps, balances) — receive your Solana address and transactions.
  • GeckoTerminal (token data) — receives standard request data such as your IP address.
  • your wallet provider (MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom…) — under its own privacy policy.
  • Supabase (only if you sign in) — your wallet address, username, theses, likes and synced settings.
  • Google Fonts and public code CDNs — standard request data.

blockchain transactions are public by nature and can be linked to your wallet address by anyone.

hosting

the site is served as static files. the hosting provider may keep standard access logs.

children

thrive is not intended for anyone under 18.

contact

questions about privacy: contact us through northpointcapital.io.
